About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Technology at Zhejiang Normal University, supervised by Prof. Jia Zhu. I am also a Lecturer at Jinhua Advanced Research Institute. Previously, I received my B.S. and M.S. degrees in Computer Science and Technology from Changsha University of Science and Technology, supervised by Prof. Jianming Zhang.

My research lies at the intersection of Computer Vision and Multimodal Artificial Intelligence. I develop methods, benchmarks, and systems that enable machines to understand, organize, and reason over complex visual and instructional environments.

Research Focus

Multimodal Intelligence for Understanding and Reasoning

My current research explores multimodal foundation models, video understanding, and knowledge-centric reasoning, with the goal of building intelligent systems that transform visual experiences into structured knowledge and actionable insights.
