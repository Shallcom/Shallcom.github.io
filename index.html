Multimodal AI · Computer Vision · Educational Technology

Multimodal intelligence for understanding, organizing, and reasoning over instructional video.

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Technology at Zhejiang Normal University, advised by Prof. Jia Zhu, and a Lecturer at Jinhua Advanced Research Institute.

My research lies at the intersection of computer vision and multimodal artificial intelligence. I develop methods, benchmarks, and systems for educational video understanding, retrieval-augmented generation, and knowledge-centric multimodal reasoning, while maintaining a long-standing interest in visual object tracking. Previously, I received my B.S. and M.S. degrees in Computer Science and Technology from Changsha University of Science and Technology, advised by Prof. Jianming Zhang.

Research profile

Research Interests

A focused research program connecting multimodal perception, structured knowledge, and intelligent educational applications.

Educational Video Understanding

Multimodal methods, benchmarks, and evaluation frameworks for understanding instructional content and pedagogical processes.

Retrieval-Augmented Multimodal AI

Retrieval and generation methods that connect audiovisual content with relevant context and knowledge.

Knowledge Graphs & Structured Reasoning

Knowledge-centric representations for organizing multimodal concepts, events, relations, and supporting information.

Visual Object Tracking

Robust and efficient visual tracking under appearance variation, target deformation, and challenging scenes.
